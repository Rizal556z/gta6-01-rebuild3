**Cinder Bay** — an original open-world crime game in the browser. You are Dax Mercer, a getaway driver fresh out of the Cinder Bay docks. Explore a procedural city on foot and by car, steal parked and driven cars, outrun the Bay Police across a 0–5 star wanted system, shoot it out with the rival Ashford crew, and run four story missions for fixer Vera Kaine — from a first car theft to the Bay Heist — with cash payouts, a circular minimap, synthesized WebAudio sound (engine, gunshots, sirens, noir bass), and localStorage saves with Continue after reload. All content is original: no GTA names, characters, maps, art or music. Built with vendored three.js, fully offline at runtime.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to move/drive, E to steal/enter/exit a car, F or Click to shoot, Space for handbrake, Shift to sprint, P to pause, M to mute.
